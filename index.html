        How to play

        1. One chicken is hiding in one of the coops. You can't see which one.
        2. Each night, search a few coops. Tap coops to pick them, then press Search.
        3. If you miss, the chicken moves to a neighboring coop. It always moves and never stays put. Knights and super roosters move differently.
        4. Amber coops show everywhere it could be. Shrink that area to nothing and there's nowhere left to hide. You've trapped it!

        Hover over a coop to see where a chicken there could go next, and keep an eye on tomorrow's forecast. Can you match par, the fewest nights possible?
